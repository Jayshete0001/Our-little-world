Our very first movie date 🍿

27 December 2025

On 27 December 2025, we decided to watch Hereditary together on Kosmi. And honestlyyyy… that movie was so boring. 🤧😭

But the best part was Kosmi itself being a little troublemaker. 😭 Every now and then an error would pop up, the movie would stop, and we had to start it again and search for the exact scene where we had left off. AGAIN. And AGAIN. Likeeee, we were putting in so much effort just to finish one movie. 🤧😂

And still, I find this memory so cute. The movie may not have been worth all that restarting, but that time with you absolutely was. I think we accidentally made the technical errors more entertaining than the actual film. 😭💕

I want to watch soooo many more movies with you, Pilluu. Horror, comedy, romance, animated movies, the good ones and even the boring ones. I want your reactions, our random comments, our silly arguments about the movie and all our little movie nights.

And I want to keep doing this until we don’t need Kosmi or any app to watch together — until you’re sitting right next to me, stealing the blanket, and we can pause the movie just to talk. I want so many movie nights with you, until we finally get to be together in the same place, living all these little moments for real. 🥹

The movie was boringgg… but my favourite part was you. Always you. ♡
